What is the minimum wholesale order?

20 pieces in total. You can mix products, colours and sizes to reach it.

Can you print or embroider our logo?

Yes. Screen printing and embroidery are available on most products. Send your logo file after we call and we'll share a mock-up before production.

How long does a wholesale order take?

In-stock items usually ship within 3–5 working days. Custom or printed orders usually take 10–15 working days after you approve the mock-up.

How do I pay?

An advance confirms the order, and the rest is paid on delivery. Bank transfer, bKash and Nagad are accepted.
