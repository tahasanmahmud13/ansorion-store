Questions

Frequently asked

How do I know my order went through?

You'll see your order number on screen right after checkout, and we'll call you to confirm. You can track it any time on this page.

Can I change or cancel my order?

Yes, until it ships. Send us a message with your order number or tell us when we call to confirm.

Do the colours look the same in real life?

We match colours as closely as we can, but screens vary. If a colour is very different from the photo, you can exchange it.

How should I wash ANSORION clothes?

Wash inside out in cold water with similar colours, don't bleach, and dry in shade. Iron on medium heat, not directly on prints.

Do you have a physical store?

We're online for now. Follow our Facebook page for pop-up events and new drops.
